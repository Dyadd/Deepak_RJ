A note on reading these tabs

Read left to right. The thin, high e string is on top; the thick, low E is on the bottom. Numbers are frets, and 0 is an open string. Notes aligned vertically are played together. Each column is one eighth note; count “1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &”. Hammer-ons, pull-offs and slides split their column into two sixteenths. A dash means no new attack; the feel note tells you when to sustain or leave silence.

These are original practice sketches, not song transcriptions. “Flow” assumes you’re comfortable with barre chords and basic fingerstyle. “Stretch” adds position shifts, independent voices, or denser voicings. The click marks quarter notes, with a higher tone on beat 1.
